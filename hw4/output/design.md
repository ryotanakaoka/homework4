# Website Design Changes

## Yale-inspired visual system

The site now uses deep Yale-inspired navy, restrained collegiate blue, warm cream, white, and a muted gold accent. Navy creates a dependable campus-store anchor, blue supports links and interactive states, cream keeps the page welcoming, and gold highlights key actions without overwhelming the product imagery.

## Typography and hierarchy

Headlines use a stronger serif treatment while body copy remains clean and readable. Eyebrows, headings, descriptions, prices, and navigation now have clearer contrast and spacing, helping customers scan the collection and understand the next action quickly.

## Product cards and images

Cards have subtle borders, soft elevation, consistent image framing, and a small lift/zoom interaction on hover. The product remains the focus while the motion gives feedback that the card is clickable. Detail pages use the same restrained image treatment for continuity.

## Interaction polish

Buttons, navigation links, size options, account actions, and chat product matches have gentle color and movement transitions. These effects communicate interaction without slowing down the shopping flow or creating visual noise.

## Chat interface

The floating chat button now fits the gold accent system, while the chat window uses a navy header, cleaner borders, readable message spacing, and compact product-match cards. This makes the assistant feel like part of the store and makes returned product recommendations easier to recognize and open.
